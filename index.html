How I approach engineering

I learn best by breaking a problem into smaller parts, understanding the constraints, and figuring out how those parts can work together.

When I work on a project, I try to connect the goal to the system that could achieve it: what each component needs to do, how the components interact, and what needs to be tested or improved. I am continuing to build my experience with design, programming, and hands-on engineering.

My next goal is to keep developing these skills through college-level engineering study, collaborative projects, and opportunities to learn from experienced engineers.

Project 01 · Concept · NASA/Engineering Club · 2025–2026

Lunar Ejecting Robot Concept

Problem. Landings on the Moon need to be documented, and astronauts need support in a harsh environment. We worked on a robot concept that could capture high-definition images of rocket landings and assist astronaut operations.

My contribution. I collaborated with the club to turn a mission objective into a robotic concept, thinking through how the hardware, robotics, imaging, and system requirements relate to each other.

A concept and collaboration project. It was not built, deployed, or used by NASA.

Robotics · Systems thinking · Imaging · Teamwork

Project 02 · Concept · Mars Artemis Mission concept

Mars Trash Ejector

Problem. A crewed space environment needs a practical way to dispose of waste without compromising the airlock.

My contribution. I worked on an airlock system and designed components of the ejection system, considering how the mechanism functions, what constraints it has, and how its parts interact.

A concept project. It was not deployed on Mars and is not an official NASA deliverable.

Mechanical design · Airlock systems · Mechanisms

Project 03 · Competition · FIRST Robotics Team 428 · 2024–2025

FIRST Robotics Competition

Role. Programming sub-team, about 50 hours. I worked on Java robot software, including autonomous and teleoperated control systems for the 2024 competition.

Here the software has to work with the physical robot: driver-controlled and autonomous behavior, coordination with hardware, and testing so the robot behaves dependably. I worked within a multidisciplinary team.

Java · Autonomous control · Teleoperation · Testing
